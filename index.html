Documented Timeline and Origin of the HAIL Tactical Medical Communications Capability

Purpose

This page documents the factual timeline and origin of the HAIL tactical medical communications capability, which I conceived and documented prior to the formation of R.A.I.N. Technologies LLC. It is published to clarify provenance using primary-source documentation. Subsequent public materials associated with HAIL and R.A.I.N. Technologies LLC, including statements by Stone Hazlett, have attributed origination elsewhere; this record presents the underlying timeline and documentation so that provenance can be evaluated directly.

This is not an opinion piece. It is a factual record.

Timeline

2017–2019: Conception and Documentation

Between 2017 and 2019, I conceived and documented the foundational concept, system architecture, UX designs, and workflows for a tactical medical communications capability. This work included:

  • End-to-end architecture concepts for medical data at the edge
  • Workflow design for collecting, transmitting, and visualizing medical information
  • Integration concepts for situational awareness systems
  • Documentation captured in handwritten notebooks and diagrams

The original materials exist as contemporaneous records, including photographs with intact metadata. These materials predate the formation of any company associated with the later commercialization of similar capabilities.

June 5, 2019: Formation of R.A.I.N. Technologies LLC

R.A.I.N. Technologies LLC was formed on June 5, 2019.

The publicly filed Articles of Organization contain no language assigning or transferring intellectual property to the company.

June 2019: Governance Documents and Initial Meeting

At formation, RAIN adopted an Operating Agreement and held its initial meeting.

  • Contains no invention assignment clause
  • Contains no work-for-hire language
  • Lists no intellectual property as contributed capital

Schedule D (member contributions) is blank.

August 1, 2019: Resignation

Effective August 1, 2019, I resigned as a Member/Manager of R.A.I.N. Technologies LLC pursuant to Section 6.2.3 of the Operating Agreement.

  • Transfers no intellectual property
  • Contains no assignment or license
  • Contains no release of rights
  • Was accepted as written by the remaining members

This resignation affected role and governance status only.

2019–Present: Commercialization and Attribution

Following my departure, RAIN developed and marketed a product later known as “HAIL,” reflecting a similar foundational architecture and workflow model to the concepts I documented prior to the company’s formation.

In subsequent public materials and statements, origination of the concept has been attributed to others. I dispute that attribution based on the documented timeline and the absence of any written assignment or transfer of my pre-existing intellectual property.

In private correspondence, RAIN leadership asserted that the company owned the intellectual property “since inception” and that I “relinquished ownership” upon departure. Those assertions are not supported by the documentary record below, and the underlying ownership and attribution issues remain unresolved.

The underlying correspondence is not published here in order to preserve privacy and to keep this record focused on primary-source documentation. It can be made available to appropriate professional audiences, including legal counsel, as needed.
